Advance copies

Digital advance copies of The Joseon Retrospective go out from 30 August 2026, ahead of publication on 20 October. Print proofs are very limited and go to trade reviewers, bookshops and library systems first.

Requests are read by the author, not a queue. If the book isn’t right for your shelf or your readers, say so — no hard feelings, and no follow-up.

What we ask in return

An honest review, posted wherever you normally post — ideally in the fortnight around publication. Goodreads and your retailer of choice help most. A star rating and a line or two is plenty; a full write-up is a gift.

Please don’t quote from the galley text itself — it isn’t final.

Also available

The book club kit, library one-sheet, bookseller one-sheet, and full press kit — all free to download and circulate. Interviews, guest posts and Q&As are welcome; just get in touch.
